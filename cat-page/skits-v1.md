# Cat page: character sheet and first 10 skits

No words in the videos. Every joke comes from actions, expressions and sound.

## 1. The character: Mango

Paste this block, unchanged, into **every** prompt. Keeping it identical is what keeps the cat looking the same across clips.

> **CHARACTER:** a chunky adult orange tabby cat named Mango, with a bright white chest, white muzzle and four white paws, round amber eyes, a small pink nose, and a distinctive slightly folded tip on his left ear. He wears a thin red collar with a small gold bell. His face is very expressive.

**Style block** (also goes in every prompt):

> **STYLE:** photorealistic smartphone video, vertical 9:16, cozy warm apartment with beige sofa, light wood floor and white kitchen tiles, soft natural daylight, slight handheld camera movement, shallow depth of field. Audio: natural room sound and real cat sounds only, no music, no speech, no on-screen text.

Why these choices:
- **Orange and white tabby:** the account you showed uses a Persian, so this one is clearly different.
- **Folded ear tip and red collar:** two small marks that make him recognizable as one specific cat.
- **Hands only for humans:** show hands, legs or someone from behind, never a face. (Inference) AI-generated human faces are where viewers most often spot a fake, and they take attention away from the cat.

## 2. How to use this in Flow

1. **First, make a reference image of Mango** from the character block alone, and keep the best one. (Unverified) Flow can use reference images, sometimes called "ingredients," to keep a character consistent. If yours has that option, add this image to every shot.
2. **For each shot,** paste: CHARACTER block + STYLE block + the shot prompt below.
3. **Generate 2 to 4 versions** and keep the one with the clearest expression.
4. **For two-shot skits,** join the clips in any editor. CapCut or Instagram's own editor are both fine.
5. **Music is optional.** Adding a trending Instagram sound on top usually suits this format.

(Unverified) Flow clips are usually about 8 seconds. Every skit below is planned in 8-second shots.

## 3. The skits

### Skit 1: The Food Review
**Shot A:** A human hand places a small bowl of dry cat food on the kitchen floor. Mango walks up, sniffs it once, freezes, then slowly lifts his head and stares straight into the camera with a flat, deeply unimpressed expression. He holds the stare. Sound: the bowl clinking, one short sniff, then silence.
**Shot B:** Mango slowly lifts one white paw, places it on the rim of the bowl and, while keeping eye contact with the camera, pushes the bowl across the floor until it slides out of frame. Sound: the bowl scraping, a distant clatter.
**Caption:** 🍽️⭐☆☆☆☆

### Skit 2: The Expensive Bed
**Shot A:** A brand new plush round cat bed sits in the middle of the living room floor. Mango walks past it without looking, steps into a small cardboard box beside it, squeezes his whole body in until he overflows the edges, and slowly blinks at the camera, completely content. Sound: cardboard creaking, a soft purr.
**Caption:** 💸🛏️ vs 📦

### Skit 3: The Laptop
**Shot A:** An open laptop on a wooden desk, human hands typing. Mango steps onto the desk, walks directly across the keyboard, then lies down on it, covering it completely, and looks up at the camera with calm satisfaction. Sound: keyboard clicks, a long string of error beeps from the laptop.
**Caption:** ⌨️🐈 work is cancelled

### Skit 4: The Replacement
**Shot A:** A human sits on the beige sofa hugging a large plush toy cat. In the doorway behind, Mango sits and watches, his eyes narrowing slowly, his tail flicking once. Sound: soft room tone, one quiet low growl.
**Shot B:** Mango walks over, hops onto the sofa, calmly pushes the plush toy off the sofa with one paw, then curls up in exactly the spot where the toy was, looking at the camera. Sound: soft thud of the toy landing, a satisfied purr.
**Caption:** 👀➡️😼

### Skit 5: 5 AM
**Shot A:** A dark bedroom lit by early blue dawn light. A person is asleep under a duvet, seen from behind. Mango sits on the pillow beside their head, staring intensely at them without moving, his eyes slightly glowing in the dim light. He slowly raises one paw and taps the person's head twice. Sound: quiet breathing, a clock ticking, two soft taps, then one loud demanding meow.
**Caption:** ⏰5:00

### Skit 6: The Vacuum
**Shot A:** A round robot vacuum moves slowly across the light wood floor toward Mango. He puffs up his fur, arches his back and side-steps dramatically with wide eyes. Sound: robot vacuum hum, one startled hiss.
**Shot B:** The robot vacuum glides across the floor with Mango now sitting on top of it like a king, calm, eyes half closed, being carried slowly across the room. Sound: vacuum hum, gentle purring.
**Caption:** 😾➡️👑

### Skit 7: The Mirror
**Shot A:** Mango walks past a tall floor mirror, notices his reflection, jumps sideways in shock with his ears flat, then slowly creeps back toward the mirror and taps it with one paw while staring suspiciously. Sound: claws on the floor, a startled chirp, a soft tap on glass.
**Shot B:** Mango sits in front of the mirror, turns his head side to side as if admiring himself, then sits up tall and proud. Sound: a slow purr.
**Caption:** 🪞🤨➡️😌

### Skit 8: Bath Time
**Shot A:** A bathroom with a running bathtub. Mango sits at the doorway, sees the water, and his expression turns to slow betrayal: wide eyes, ears turning back, his whole body leaning away. Sound: running water, one long offended meow.
**Shot B:** A dark space under a bed. Only Mango's two amber eyes are visible, glowing, staring out at the camera without blinking. Sound: complete silence, then a tiny low growl.
**Caption:** 🛁❌

### Skit 9: The Treat Bag
**Shot A:** Close-up of a human hand gently crinkling a shiny bag of cat treats in the kitchen. Sound: loud crinkling.
**Shot B:** A long hallway. Mango sprints full speed toward the camera, skids on the wooden floor, slides past the kitchen doorway, scrambles back and sits perfectly upright in front of the camera with huge, innocent eyes. Sound: fast paws, claws skidding, a sweet small meow.
**Caption:** 🔊🛍️➡️🏃‍♂️💨

### Skit 10: The Closed Door
**Shot A:** A closed white bedroom door seen from the hallway. One white paw reaches under the gap and waves around, searching. Sound: scratching, a muffled meow.
**Shot B:** The door opens. Mango sits in the doorway, looks inside the room, looks at the camera, then turns around and walks away without entering. Sound: door creak, one short dismissive chirp.
**Caption:** 🚪😤➡️😐
