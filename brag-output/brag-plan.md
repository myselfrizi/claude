# Test reel: "Biscuit has opinions"

**What it is:** A test reel for a cartoon cat comedy page. It checks whether code-drawn animation can carry the format before any AI video setup.

**Character:** Biscuit, a cream cat with orange stripes on his forehead and a pink nose. He's dramatic and treats small household changes as crimes.

**Angle:** A tiny change, an outsized reaction. The household drama, played completely straight.

**Hook:** "POV: you moved his food bowl 2cm" is on screen from the first frame.

**Tone:** Default (punchy, playful, clean), with a mock-thriller middle section.

**Format:** Vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, 13s.

**Visual identity:** Pastel kitchen (pink-cream wall, warm beige floor). Thick dark outlines. Baloo 2 ExtraBold for captions in white rounded boxes.

## Storyboard

| Time | Scene | On screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.6s | Hook | Biscuit sits calmly beside his bowl. Caption pops in. A hand drags the bowl 2cm to the right. | Light pluck melody, soft bass |
| 2.6–4.8s | Reaction | A "2cm" marker appears. Eyes go huge, ears flatten, mouth drops open. | Music stops, record scratch, low minor sting |
| 4.8–8.0s | The threat | Camera slams into his face. Red vignette and a slight shake. Speech bubble: "I will be contacting my lawyer." | Low drone, two dramatic hits |
| 8.0–11.0s | Justice | Camera pulls back. His paw drags the bowl back to its original spot. Smug closed eyes. Caption: "Order has been restored." | Melody returns, sparkle chime |
| 11.0–13.0s | Outro | Title card: "Biscuit has opinions." / "Follow for daily drama" | Melody resolves on a final chord |
